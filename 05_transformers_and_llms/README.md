# 🤖 Transformers & Large Language Models (LLMs)

This section introduces the core concepts of **Transformer architectures** and **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, which power modern AI systems such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Llama, and many other state-of-the-art language models.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To understand how Transformers revolutionized Natural Language Processing by replacing recurrent architectures with attention mechanisms, and to explore how Large Language Models are trained, fine-tuned, and applied to real-world AI applications.

### Large Language Models

### Modern LLM Techniques

- Prompt Engineering
